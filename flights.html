Before booking

  • Visa: apply through the French visa centre as planned and list the beach city on the itinerary. Turkey is visa-free for Kazakh passports and outside Schengen, so a single-entry visa covers the Antalya plan.
  • Tickets: Almaty → Paris becomes a one-way. Check Pegasus multi-city (ALA → ORY, AYT → ALA) as one booking. Pegasus flies both Antalya and Almaty.
  • Bags: the cheapest Transavia, easyJet, Vueling and Pegasus fares include only an underseat bag. Add one checked bag per person to every quote.
  • Self-transfer: on two separate tickets, leave 3 h or more in Istanbul. IST and SAW are different airports, 1.5 h apart.
  • Weather: mid-April sea is 15–18 °C everywhere here. Expect sunny walks, not swimming. Antalya is the warmest at 20–24 °C, Barcelona about 18 °C.
  • When: watch prices now, buy Jan–Feb 2027 (see the Timeline sheet in the budget spreadsheet).
