Before you fly

  • Bags: 25 kg checked bag each is included both ways. For Paris → Barcelona, cheap fares are cabin-bag only, so add bags when you buy.
  • Ankara: both changes are international to international, so you normally stay airside. Turkey is visa-free for Kazakh passports anyway.
  • Visa: 9 of the 12 nights are in France, so apply at the French visa centre in February. You enter Schengen in Paris and leave from Barcelona. Insurance must cover 16–29 April.
  • Schedule changes: airlines re-time spring flights after the slot conference (17–19 Nov). Watch for AJet emails.
