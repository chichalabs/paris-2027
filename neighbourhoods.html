Paris on a budget - where to stay

Paris / 13-21 April 2027 / 8 nights, two people

Where to actually stay

You asked for the Marais or the Eiffel Tower. Those are the two most expensive places in the city to sleep. Everything coloured below is a way to get the same trip for EUR 90-140 a night instead of double that. Hover anything to link the map to the notes.

The shortlist

Prices are what a decent studio or aparthotel should cost per night in mid-April. Walk times are to the middle of the Marais.

11eOberkampf / Bastille

EUR 90-140/10-20 min walk to the Marais

The recommendation. You get Marais evenings without the Marais room rate, and it is where Parisians actually eat and drink rather than where tour groups get photographed. Oberkampf, Rue de la Roquette and Popincourt are the streets to search.

12eBercy / Aligre

EUR 85-130/15 min walk to the Marais

Quieter and cheaper again. The Marche d'Aligre is one of the last real market streets in central Paris, and the Coulee Verte is a planted railway viaduct you can walk the length of. Residential in the good way.

3eHaut Marais

EUR 120-170/You are already in it

Genuinely the Marais, but the upper half around Arts et Metiers, so noticeably cheaper than the 4th below it. Spend the extra here if the Marais itself is non-negotiable for her.

10eCanal St-Martin

EUR 80-120/15 min walk to the Marais

Canal Saint-Martin is cheap and has real atmosphere, but stay south and east of the water. Anything within a few blocks of Gare du Nord, Gare de l'Est or Chateau d'Eau is where the suspiciously cheap listings live, and you will not enjoy the walk home.

15eVaugirard

EUR 85-125/Across the river from the tower

The honest way to wake up near the Eiffel Tower. Residential, safe, a fraction of 7th prices, and some streets have a tower view from the window. The trade is that it is quiet, so you will be taking the metro out for dinner.

7eEiffel / Invalides

EUR 180-260/Skip it

The obvious choice and the wrong one. Past the price, this is an embassy and ministry district: dead after dark, almost nowhere to eat. You can see the tower from half the city, so you do not need to sleep underneath it. The 4th, 6th, 1st and 8th fail the same test.

18eMontmartre

cheap/Not worth the saving

Montmartre proper is lovely and expensive. The cheap listings are north of it, around Barbes, La Chapelle and Chateau Rouge, and the 19th near Stalingrad is the same trade. Cheap for reasons you will feel at midnight.

One caveat worth taking seriously: this is drawn at arrondissement level, and streets vary enormously inside them. The 10th is the clearest case, where four blocks decides whether you have booked a canal or a station forecourt. Once you have a shortlist, check the exact address rather than trusting the colour. Boundaries are the City of Paris open-data set; the Seine is derived from where the left-bank and right-bank arrondissements meet.
